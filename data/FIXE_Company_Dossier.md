# FIXE Company Dossier
## Comprehensive Overview of FIXE Restaurant Financial Operations Platform

**Website:** https://www.getmyfixe.com  
**Prepared:** August 2026

# Executive Summary

FIXE is a technology-enabled financial operations company focused exclusively on the restaurant industry. Rather than positioning itself as a bookkeeping firm, FIXE acts as an outsourced finance department, combining experienced restaurant accountants, modern cloud software, automation, and restaurant-specific reporting into a single monthly subscription.

Its value proposition is simple:

> **Give restaurant operators timely, accurate financial information so they can make better operational decisions while spending less time managing accounting.**

Unlike many accounting firms that simply produce financial statements, FIXE integrates operational systems—including POS, payroll, inventory, delivery platforms, accounts payable, and accounting software—to create a single source of financial truth for restaurant operators.

# Pricing

## Essentials — $690/month/location
Designed for restaurants up to approximately $2.5M annual revenue.

Includes:
- Dedicated account manager
- Weekly KPI reporting
- Monthly P&L and Balance Sheet
- Bank reconciliations
- Sales tax, property tax and business license filings
- Vendor payment management
- POS integration
- Third-party delivery reconciliation
- Chargeback dispute management
- Cloud dashboard

## Pro+ — $830/month/location
Designed for restaurants up to approximately $7.5M annual revenue.

Adds:

- Inventory adjustments
- Intercompany accounting
- Quarterly financial reviews
- Flexible integrations
- More sophisticated accounting workflows
